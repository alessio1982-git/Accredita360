# Nano Banana Video & Audio Demo Generator for Accredita360

This skill provides full automation workflows to generate, capture, and export high-definition video presentations with synchronized Italian voiceover for the Accredita360 health compliance platform.

## Workflow Overview
1. **Interactive Demo Engine (`video-demo.html`)**:
   - 10 automated slides covering 2026 Sicilian health accreditation regulations (DA 890/2023, DA 1150/2025, DA 1151/2025) and the 7 MAMB Technical Cards.
   - Built-in Speech Synthesis with medical phonetic tuning and synchronized teleprompter subtitles.
   - Integrated MediaRecorder for 1-click in-browser video export (.webm / .mp4).

2. **Nano Banana Pro & Gemini Veo Prompt Compilation**:
   - Structured JSON payloads for cloud generation of promotional video reels, animated UI mockups, and avatar lip-sync.

3. **Audio Configuration**:
   - Language: `it-IT`
   - Optimal Rate: `0.85` (relaxed & clear)
   - Pronunciation maps for acronyms (MAMB, ADI, RSA, OTA, ASP, SSR).
